Adray Core's best customer is the manager who owns the online store's revenue number — usually a Head of Ecommerce or Growth at a brand doing about $1M–$20M a year online. They don't want more data; they want to know where to push their team. The sale starts with a free diagnostic, with no contract for now.

## Who buys, by company stage

Growth-stage brands are the core; small stores and enterprise are real but secondary markets with different sales motions.

| Stage | Online revenue | Monthly ad spend | Who signs | What their month looks like | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | under ~$1M/yr | under $5k | Founder, who also runs the ads | Wants a quick answer on which ads work; very price-sensitive; will try free tools first | Low. Not a fit until onboarding is mostly automatic |
| **Growth** | **~$1M–$20M/yr** | **$5k–$150k** | **Head of Ecommerce / Growth** | Owns a monthly revenue target, platforms disagree, reports to CEO; small team, often one agency | **Best fit. All three promises matter** |
| Enterprise | $20M+/yr or omnichannel | $150k+ | Ecommerce or Digital Director, CMO; procurement | Agencies run media, VTEX or custom stack, board-level reporting, long approval cycles | Good, but slow; needs the team |
| Agencies | 10–50 ecommerce clients | Across clients | Agency owner / partner | Proving results to each client; reporting eats margin | Good channel; priced per client |

Revenue and spend bands are working assumptions, to be checked against current clients' real numbers.

**ICP floor (decided Oct 2026):** at least $5k a month in ad spend. At that floor, the 15,000 MXN price is about 17% of ad spend, so sell against the cost of a team rather than the ad budget, and watch close rates for brands spending under ~$15k.

**Two Core buckets:** mid-market (up to 200k GA4 sessions a month, 15,000 MXN, no contract) and enterprise (consistently above 200k sessions; a separate product, go-to-market and service).

## The buying team

One person signs, but four or five decide; each one hears a different promise first.

| Role | Part in the deal | What they're trying to achieve | Promise that lands | What they need to hear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Head of Ecommerce / Growth** | Buyer | Hit the monthly revenue target and defend the budget | All three | "You'll know by day 10 whether you'll hit the month, and what to change." |
| **Performance marketing manager** | Champion or blocker | Scale what works, cut what doesn't, spend less time on reports | What's working | "See which campaigns really sell, checked against your orders, without leaving the tools you use." |
| **CFO / Finance** | Approver, and a second buyer | Trust marketing's numbers; predict the month; protect margin | What's making money + what to do next | "Revenue and margin by channel that match your books, and a forecast you can plan against." |
| **CRO / UX / Ecommerce manager** | User | Raise conversion rate and order value | What's working (behavior) | "Every visit reviewed: where shoppers get stuck and what makes them buy." |
| **Merchandising / Inventory** | Influencer | Keep the products that sell in stock | What's making money | "Which products carry the store, and what a stock-out will cost this month." |
| **CEO / Founder** | Final say in growth stage | Grow profitably | What to do next | "One place that tells you what's working and what to do about it." |
| **IT / Web developer** | Gatekeeper | Protect site speed and security | — | "One script or two clicks in Tag Manager; no impact on speed; your data stays yours." |
| **Agency running the ads** | Influencer (can help or resist) | Keep the client, show results | What's working | "Proof your work drives real sales, not just platform numbers." |

**The biggest shift from today's positioning:** Finance is a buyer, not just an approver. "How much will we sell this month?" is a finance question, and it justifies a higher price than attribution alone.

**Customer evidence:** a client told us, "I don't need to see all the data, I need to know how to push my team and where." The buyer is a manager directing people, so every recommendation should name who on their team acts on it.

## What they search for, and when

Buyers rarely search for "attribution software"; they search for the problem in front of them, usually right after a bad month.

| They're thinking | They search (Spanish / English) | Promise to answer with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta and GA4 don't agree | "por qué no coinciden Meta y Google Analytics" / "Meta vs GA4 numbers don't match" | What's working |
| Is my ROAS real? | "ROAS real", "atribución multi-touch" / "true ROAS", "attribution tool" | What's making money |
| Looking at a known tool | "alternativa a Triple Whale", "Triple Whale VTEX" / "Triple Whale alternative" | All three, plus "works on any platform" |
| Conversion is dropping | "por qué baja mi tasa de conversión" / "why is conversion rate dropping" | What's working (behavior) |
| Planning next month | "pronóstico de ventas ecommerce", "presupuesto Hot Sale" / "ecommerce sales forecast" | What to do next |
| Which products carry us | "productos más rentables", "análisis de SKU" / "SKU profitability" | What's making money |

**Moments that trigger a search:**

- A missed monthly target, or a month that's tracking badly by week two.
- A new CEO, CFO or board member questioning marketing spend.
- Planning Hot Sale, Buen Fin or Black Friday budgets.
- Switching agencies, or bringing media in-house.
- Moving to a new store platform (Shopify, VTEX) and losing historical tracking.
- A tool renewal coming up (Triple Whale, GA4 360, an analytics agency).

The search terms above are hypotheses; validate them with keyword data before writing SEO pages.

## Sales approach

Most customers need onboarding to get value, so there's no free tier: a free diagnostic earns the meeting, and the first 30 days of a 12-month contract prove the value through onboarding milestones.

| Package | First touch | How they get value | Moment that proves it | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growth (15,000 MXN/mo) | Search, comparison pages (vs Triple Whale, Polar), referrals, agencies, inbound demo requests | Free diagnostic, then 30-day onboarding: data connected, daily story, five moves with owners | Onboarding milestones met; the team acts on the first five moves | Sales + customer success |
| Core Enterprise | Outbound to Ecommerce Directors; partners (agencies, platform partners) | Onboarding on one brand first | A finance or board meeting uses Adray numbers | Founder-led sales |
| Agency | Partner outreach, community | One client onboarded, then per client | Agency uses the daily story in a client meeting | Partnerships |

**The path from first touch to renewal (Growth tier):**

1. **Hook** with the banner: "Know where to push your team." Offer a free diagnostic.
2. **Diagnostic** (30–45 min): where platforms disagree with real sales, and the three biggest quick wins.
3. **Discovery:** their monthly target, their trigger, who on their team does what, and their monthly ad spend (floor: $5k).
4. **Sign** up at 15,000 MXN/month, with no contract and onboarding included.
5. **Onboard:** data connected by day 7, first daily story by day 14, five moves with owners by day 21, team trained by day 30.
6. **Priority reviews** as the retention engine; each ends with the next five moves.
7. **Expand** into Core Enterprise or more brands.

**Expansion signals to watch:** sessions nearing the next band, a second brand or country, or repeated requests the team handles by hand (agent candidates).

## Sales toolkit

Every conversation should end with the buyer saying the three promises back in their own words.

**Discovery questions (pick four or five):**

1. What should your team be pushing on this week, and how do you decide that today?
2. What's your revenue target this month, and how confident are you that you'll hit it?
3. When Meta, Google and your store disagree, which number do you use to decide budget?
4. Who has to agree before budget moves: you, finance, the CEO, the agency?
5. How do you find out why people visit and don't buy?
6. Which products carry most of your revenue, and what happens if they run out?
7. How do you plan a big sales event today?
8. What happened last time a month went badly, and how long did it take to find out why?

**Demo flow (15 minutes, in their data if possible):**

1. **Their daily story:** one line on how their business, marketing and technology did yesterday.
2. **Five moves with owners:** ranked by money at stake, each assigned to a role on their team.
3. **Why:** platform-reported vs real results side by side, plus one site-behavior finding in pesos.
4. **What's making money:** revenue and margin by channel and top products.
5. **Where it lives:** the same answer by email, Slack or WhatsApp, and in ChatGPT or Claude.
6. **Who helps:** introduce the team, the monthly priority reviews.

**Objections and answers:**

| They say | Answer with |
| --- | --- |
| "We already have GA4 / Triple Whale." | They report what happened. Adray checks it against real sales, explains why, and tells you what to do next. |
| "Our agency handles reporting." | Adray gives you and your agency the same true number, so you can judge their work on real sales. |
| "It's expensive." | Compare it to the budget it helps you move. Moving 5% of $50k/mo to what actually sells pays for it several times. |
| "We don't have time to set it up." | Connecting takes minutes; the team does the rest with you. |
| "Is our data safe?" | Read-only access, data stays yours, DPA available. (Only say this once the security gaps are closed.) |
| "We're not on Shopify." | Works on WooCommerce, VTEX, Magento and custom sites; larger stacks go through Enterprise setup. |

## Lead gen in brief

The same three promises apply to service businesses (universities, B2B, real estate, auto); only the anchor moves from the store order to the closed deal in the CRM.

|  | Ecommerce | Lead gen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer | Head of Ecommerce / Growth | Commercial or Marketing Director |
| Second buyer | CFO | Sales Director |
| Core pain | Platforms claim more sales than the store made | "Marketing says 340 leads, sales says 12 deals" |
| What's working | Click to verified order | Click to form, WhatsApp chat and CRM deal |
| What to do next | Month forecast, stock risk | Pipeline forecast, lead response speed |
| Setup effort | Low (store + ads) | Higher (CRM, WhatsApp) — always a Growth tier or above |
